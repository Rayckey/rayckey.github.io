01 / ABOUT ME

Weiqi Wang

I’m Weiqi Wang, a robotics researcher and engineer. My work spans robot learning, model-based control, manipulation, and physical human–robot interaction.

I completed my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering at UCLA in September 2026. My dissertation, Embodiment-Aware Task Realization for Human–Robot Coexistence, connects how robots are built with what they can do in the world.
