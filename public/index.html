01 / ABOUT ME

Weiqi Wang

I’m Weiqi Wang, a robotics researcher and engineer. I work on robotic manipulation and locomotion, combining robot learning, motion planning, and model-based control.

I completed my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering at UCLA in September 2026. My dissertation, Embodiment-Aware Task Realization for Human–Robot Coexistence, connects how robots are built with what they can do in the world.

MANIPULATION + ROBOT LEARNINGUnder review · 2026

SUN: Agentic Robot
Policy Learning

I built Kuafu, an agentic robot-learning harness for multi-stage robot manipulation. It connects model predictive control, imitation learning through behavior cloning, and residual reinforcement learning. I also trained visual manipulation policies and developed a sim-to-real deployment pipeline for Franka FR3 and Kinova Gen3.
